## 2. The short version

- sampta.org uses **no cookies**, **no analytics** and **no tracking**.
- The website loads no fonts, scripts or other content from third parties.
- We only store your email address if you **subscribe to the mailing list** yourself or **write to us**.
- You can unsubscribe and ask us to delete your data at any time.

## 3. Visiting the website

### Hosting (Netlify)

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., San Francisco, USA. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to the server: your IP address, the date and time, the page you opened, and your browser and operating system. This is necessary to show you the website and to keep it secure.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in running a secure and working website).

### Content delivery and security (Cloudflare)

The domain sampta.org and its DNS are managed by Cloudflare, Inc., San Francisco, USA. Website traffic passes through Cloudflare's network, which protects the site against attacks and makes it load faster. Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical connection data for this.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a secure and fast website).

### Data transfers outside the EU

Netlify and Cloudflare are US companies, so data may be processed in the USA. These transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 4. Contacting us by email

If you write to an @sampta.org address (for example info@sampta.org), your message is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the people who look after the website and the conference series. We use your email address and your message only to answer you. We delete them when they are no longer needed.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering your questions), or Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your message is about a contract.

## 5. Mailing list

### What we send

The SampTA mailing list is a low-volume announcement list. We send save-the-dates, calls for papers, registration openings and important deadlines for SampTA conferences.

### Sign-up and double opt-in

You can subscribe through the sign-up form linked on this website. After you sign up, you receive an email with a confirmation link. You are only added to the list after you click this link (double opt-in). We store your email address, the time of sign-up and confirmation, and your IP address at that time, so that we can prove your consent.

**Legal basis:** your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR.

### Our newsletter provider (Brevo)

We use Brevo, a service of Sendinblue SAS, 17 rue Salneuve, 75017 Paris, France. Brevo is a company based in the EU. It stores the subscriber list and sends our emails on our behalf, based on a data processing agreement. Brevo can record whether an email was delivered, opened, or a link in it was clicked.

### Spam protection on the sign-up form

The sign-up form is hosted by Brevo and uses Google reCAPTCHA (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to block automated spam sign-ups. reCAPTCHA processes technical data such as your IP address and browser information, which may also be sent to Google LLC in the USA.

### Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email, or by writing to [info@sampta.org](mailto:info@sampta.org). This withdraws your consent for the future. We then remove your address from the list.

## 6. Editors of the website

Conference organizers who edit parts of this website sign in with a GitHub account. This only concerns editors, not normal visitors.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- get information about the data we store about you (Art. 15),
- correct wrong data (Art. 16),
- have your data deleted (Art. 17),
- restrict how we process your data (Art. 18),
- receive your data in a common format (Art. 20),
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
- withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3)).

To use these rights, write to [info@sampta.org](mailto:info@sampta.org).

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

## 8. Changes to this notice

We update this notice when the website or our services change. The current version is always available on this page.
